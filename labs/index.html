Formation studies — lab

Three formations, taken apart

Each of these takes a crop formation built from circles and lines and asks what it is made of. They are on one page so they can be compared: same controls, same readouts, same discipline. Every figure resolves at once rather than being constructed step by step, nothing is moved or resized away from where the design puts it, and every pattern claim is computed and asserted before a single line of it is drawn.

How to read these. Each study plays on load and can be paused or scrubbed; scrubbing backwards genuinely un-draws the figure, because every frame is a pure function of one progress value rather than an accumulation. Studies pause themselves while scrolled out of view. All three honour prefers-reduced-motion: reduce by rendering a single finished frame with no motion at all.

On the two kinds of claim. Anything stated about a real formation — a date, a site, a circle count — comes from the research archive and is flagged where it has not been confirmed against a primary source. Anything stated about geometry is computed here, in the page, and the residuals are readable from the console: STUDIES.hexcube.pattern and STUDIES.lattice.pattern. The two are kept apart on purpose. A figure that looks like an argument should be held to the same standard as one.
